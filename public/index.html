Earth • Grounding & Holding

Grief Support & Integration

Quiet hope. A heart-centered space to honor loss, meet emotions safely, and grow around your experience at your own pace.

Grief isn't a problem to solve; it is a profound human process to move through—at your own pace, in your own way. It can show up unexpectedly as physical exhaustion, difficulty concentrating, anger, or chronic overworking.

"Pain doesn’t always fade completely, but we can learn to hold it differently, build capacity around it, and participate fully in life again."

In these sessions, you will find space to:

  • Process loss without pressure to "move on" or put up a brave face
  • Gently discharge accumulated tension and emotional fatigue
  • Honor what was lost while slowly building new meaning

Fire • Creative Feminine Expression

Pretty & Angry Girls Club

Not rage—creative fire. Transforming misunderstood emotions into vitality, clear boundaries, and authentic connection.

A series of curated group gatherings (in-person in Zurich and online) where women come together to meet one of the most suppressed and misunderstood emotions: ANGER.

Embodied Practice

Movement, breathwork, boxing elements, improv, and vocal release to move bound energy out safely.

The Experience

Shift from "I am overwhelmed" to "I feel vibrant, clear on my boundaries, and fully alive."

Inspired by the Polish saying "Złość piękności szkodzi" (Anger harms beauty)—a cultural conditioning we actively dismantle to reclaim natural vitality.

Background & Roots

About Justyna

One of the songs that stayed with me from a young age was “It’s My Life” by Bon Jovi—especially the line: “It’s my life, it’s now or never.”

Those words resonated deeply because I learned early that life is both fragile and precious. Having lost my mother as a child, I experienced the depth of loss early on, while witnessing resilience through my father’s steady presence.

"What is life? How do we not simply move through our days on autopilot, but truly participate in them?"

Bridging Corporate Performance & Somatic Presence

Alongside my practice, I serve as an HR Business Partner in a global reinsurance company in Zurich. My work bridges strategic clarity with body intelligence.
