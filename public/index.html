Grounding & Holding

Grief Support & Integration

A heart-centered space to honor loss, meet emotions safely, and grow around your experience at your own pace.

Grief isn't a problem to solve or a linear checklist; it is a profound human process to move through—at your own pace, in your own way.

"Pain doesn’t always fade completely, but we can learn to hold it differently, build capacity around it, and participate fully in life again."

In these sessions, you will find space to:

  • Process loss without pressure to "move on" or put up a brave face
  • Somatic discharge of accumulated grief tension
  • Reconnect gently with daily routine and personal agency

Background & Roots

About Justyna

One of the songs that stayed with me from a young age was “It’s My Life” by Bon Jovi—especially the line: “It’s my life, it’s now or never.”

Those words resonated deeply because I learned early that life is fragile and precious. Having lost my mother early in life, I experienced loss deeply while witnessing resilience through my father's steady presence.

"What is life? How do we not simply move through our days on autopilot, but truly participate in them?"

Bridging Corporate Performance & Somatic Presence

Alongside my practice, I serve as an HR Business Partner in Zurich. My work bridges strategic clarity with body intelligence for sustainable leadership and performance.
