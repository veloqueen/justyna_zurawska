Grounding & Holding

Grief Support & Integration

Quiet hope. A heart-centered space to honor loss, meet emotions safely, and grow around your experience at your own pace.

Grief isn't a problem to solve or a linear checklist; it is a profound human process to move through—at your own pace, in your own way. It can be quiet, numb, loud, or show up unexpectedly as physical exhaustion, difficulty concentrating, anger, or chronic overworking.

"Pain doesn’t always fade completely, but we can learn to hold it differently, build capacity around it, and participate fully in life again."

Having navigated my own deep grief since childhood following the loss of my mother, I offer a grounded, non-judgmental space. Through integrative coaching and somatic breathwork, we create conditions where you can safely release what is stored in the body and integrate loss with gentleness.

In these sessions, you will find space to:

  • Process loss without pressure to "move on" or put up a brave face
  • Somatic discharge of accumulated grief tension and emotional fatigue
  • Reconnect gently with your body, daily routine, and personal agency
  • Honor what was lost while slowly building new meaning for the future

Creative Feminine Expression

Pretty & Angry Girls Club

Not rage—creative fire. Transforming misunderstood emotions into vitality, clear boundaries, and authentic connection.

Pretty & Angry Girls Club is a series of curated group gatherings (in-person in Zurich and online) where women come together to meet one of the most suppressed and misunderstood emotions: ANGER.

Women are often taught to mask anger with politeness, perfectionism, or compliance. Here, we don’t push it away, apologize for it, or attempt to fix it. We embrace it, express it physically, and channel it together into creative energy and unshakable boundaries.

Background & Roots

About Justyna

One of the songs that stayed with me from a young age was “It’s My Life” by Bon Jovi—especially the line: “It’s my life, it’s now or never.”

Those words resonated deeply because I learned early that life is both fragile and precious. After losing my mother at a young age, I experienced the depth of loss and the ways grief changes our relationship with life. At the same time, I witnessed resilience through my father’s conscious, steady presence—showing me that we always retain agency in how we meet the world.

"What is life? How do we not simply move through our days on autopilot, but truly participate in them?"

My path has taken me from a small Polish village to international communities, degrees in Business Management and Intercultural Communication, philosophy studies, a full-stack code bootcamp, corporate HR roles in Zurich, and somatic breathwork certifications.

Bridging Corporate Performance & Somatic Presence

Having worked across multinational organisations, academia, and startups, I understand the realities of leading, performing, and adapting in demanding environments.

Before you book

Questions, answered.

Can you help me decide whether to take a promotion? +

Yes. We can look at the practical reality, what the promotion means to you, the trade-offs and your response to the options. I will not tell you which one to take.

What if I don't know what I want yet? +

That is a valid starting point. The work can begin by clarifying what has changed and what you want to pay attention to next.

Is this therapy? +

No. This is coaching and somatic work, not psychotherapy or medical treatment.
