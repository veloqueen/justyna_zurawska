My approach

Strategic thinking + felt sense + conscious choice

Important decisions are rarely only intellectual. They involve your thoughts, emotions, experiences and the way your whole system responds.

What I know

The facts. Your reality. Your options. Your practical considerations.

What I think

Your beliefs. Your assumptions. Your expectations. The stories you carry about success and what you "should" do.

What I feel

Your emotional experience. Your desires. Your fears. Your hopes.

What I sense

Your felt experience. The information available through your internal responses: openness, resistance, energy, discomfort, alignment.

What I choose

A conscious decision. A choice you understand, trust and can move forward with.

A question for this era

In a world full of information, clarity becomes a human advantage.

AI can help you explore possibilities, analyse options, organise your thoughts and ask powerful questions.

These tools are valuable. But meaningful decisions require more than information. They require perspective, reflection, understanding your patterns, connection with your lived experience and the ability to recognise what truly matters.

AI can analyse the options.
It cannot decide what is worth choosing.

As information becomes easier to access, human judgement becomes less about finding another answer — and more about knowing what matters enough to choose.

A trusted space to explore, reflect and decide.

You do not need someone to choose for you. You need a space where you can slow down, see clearly and reconnect with your own knowing.

A space where you can:

  • explore possibilities;
  • challenge assumptions;
  • understand your patterns;
  • recognise what feels aligned;
  • make a decision you can genuinely own.

Before you book

Questions, answered.

Can you help me decide whether to take a promotion?

Yes. We can look at the practical reality, what the promotion means to you, the trade-offs and your response to the options. I will not tell you which one to take.

What if I don't know what I want yet?

That is a valid starting point. The work can begin by clarifying what has changed and what you want to pay attention to next.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching and somatic work, not psychotherapy or medical treatment.
