About Justyna

Different perspectives.
One human approach.

My professional path has taken me through international education, corporate environments, intercultural work, coaching and somatic practice.

Having worked in global organisations, I understand the demands of performance, responsibility and constant adaptation. My background in Intercultural Communication informs how I understand people navigating different cultural environments.

Alongside this professional experience, I am interested in the deeper dimensions of being human: how we experience change, relate to ourselves and others, express what matters and create lives that feel meaningful.

I bring these perspectives together through strategic thinking, coaching, embodied awareness and creative experiences.
