The method

Make room for the whole decision.

Your choices are not made by your rational mind alone. We look at information, story, emotion and the body's response — then bring them into one conscious choice.

What I know

Facts, constraints, practical reality.

What I think

Assumptions, beliefs and conditioning.

What I feel

Emotions, desires, fear and excitement.

What my body says

Somatic response, tension, openness, capacity.

What I choose

A conscious decision — including the trade-offs I am willing to make.

Before you book

Questions, answered.

Can you help me decide whether to take a promotion?

Yes. We can look at the practical reality, what the promotion means to you, the trade-offs and your response to the options. I will not tell you which one to take.

What if I don't know what I want yet?

That is a valid starting point. The work can begin by clarifying what has changed and what you want to pay attention to next.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching and somatic work, not psychotherapy or medical treatment.
