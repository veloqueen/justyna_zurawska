Grounding & Holding

Grief Support & Integration

Quiet hope. A heart-centered space to honor loss, meet emotions safely, and grow around your experience at your own pace.

Grief isn't a problem to solve or a linear checklist; it is a profound human process to move through—at your own pace, in your own way. It can be quiet, numb, loud, or show up unexpectedly as physical exhaustion, difficulty concentrating, anger, or chronic overworking.

"Pain doesn’t always fade completely, but we can learn to hold it differently, build capacity around it, and participate fully in life again."

Having navigated my own deep grief since childhood following the loss of my mother, I offer a grounded, non-judgmental space. Through integrative coaching and somatic breathwork, we create conditions where you can safely release what is stored in the body and integrate loss with gentleness.

In these sessions, you will find space to:

  • Process loss without pressure to "move on" or put up a brave face
  • Somatic discharge of accumulated grief tension and emotional fatigue
  • Reconnect gently with your body, daily routine, and personal agency
  • Honor what was lost while slowly building new meaning for the future

Built on personal resilience and ICF-accredited grief coaching education (From Grief to Gratitude) alongside participation in Hope Edelman’s Motherless Daughters circles.
