Understanding The Context

Meeting You Where You Operate

Zurich reflects the world many of my clients navigate: international, fast-paced, intellectually demanding, and built on quality and responsibility.

Executive refers not to a job title or hierarchical level, but the experience of carrying complexity, making decisions, leading change, and performing under pressure.

My praxis supports people who want to meet these demands without losing clarity, presence, or themselves.

Alignment & Intent

This Work Is a Good Fit If:

Ready to shift from collecting insights to living them

You've read the books. You've listened to podcasts. Maybe you've even tried coaching or therapy. Yet you still find yourself reacting in the same situations. You're ready for change that is experienced—not just understood.

Willing to look honestly at outdated patterns

You are ready to look honestly at the thoughts, habits, and protective mechanisms that no longer serve your growth, well-being, or leadership.

Ready to listen to your body

You've ignored your body's signals for years because work came first. Now you're curious what happens when you finally listen.

Open to exploring mind & nervous system

You are open to exploring both your cognitive landscape and your nervous system—moving beyond just collecting conceptual ideas.

Ready to exit autopilot survival mode

Tired of surviving on autopilot, pushing through exhaustion, and ready to genuinely reconnect with yourself and your vitality.

Ready to step into personal agency

Ready to take complete responsibility for your life and choices without placing blame on yourself or your circumstances for where you are today.

Seeking decisions grounded in clarity

You want difficult conversations, high-stakes leadership decisions, and personal choices to come from grounded clarity instead of stress, urgency, or fear of disappointing others.

Prepared to practice and integrate

Prepared to experiment, practice somatic tools, and integrate new ways of being between our dedicated sessions.

The Dual Approach to Leadership.

A coaching and somatic program for professionals who want to:

  • ✓ Regain clarity
  • ✓ Reduce stress patterns
  • ✓ Reconnect with themselves
  • ✓ Make confident decisions
  • ✓ Create sustainable success
🧠

Strategic Coaching

Examine cognitive assumptions, decision-making habits, and perfectionist patterns driving your workload. Gain strategic clarity, break through autopilot thinking, and intentionally direct your leadership presence.

What you gain:
  • Clear framework for complex executive decision-making
  • Identification and dismantling of burnout triggers
  • Aligned leadership presence without over-explaining
  • Boundary-setting that protects your focus and energy
🫁

Somatic Breathwork & Integration

Bypass intellectual over-analysis to access the nervous system directly. Down-regulate stress responses, release accumulated physical tension armor, and embody calm authority under high pressure.

What you gain:
  • Rapid physiological de-escalation of acute stress
  • Improved heart rate variability and deeper rest
  • Release of chronic bodily holding patterns & fatigue
  • Grounded emotional stability in high-stakes environments

The Client Transformation Journey

I work with ambitious professionals who are successful on paper but feel overwhelmed, disconnected, or stuck. I help them reduce stress, gain clarity, and reconnect with their own resources so they can navigate change, make confident decisions, and create success that is sustainable.

Step 1 • Achievement

Cognitive Dominance

Sustained by intellect, hyper-vigilance, and over-working habits that created initial success but now cause strain.

Step 2 • Regulation

Somatic Realignment

Down-regulating the nervous system, releasing bodily tension armor, and clarifying core values.

Step 3 • Integration

Full Participation

Leading and living with grounded presence, authentic authority, clear boundaries, and renewed vitality.
