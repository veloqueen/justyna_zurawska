Creative Feminine Expression

Pretty & Angry Girls Club

Not rage—creative fire. Transforming misunderstood emotions into vitality, clear boundaries, and authentic connection.

Pretty & Angry Girls Club is a series of curated group gatherings (in-person in Zurich and online) where women come together to meet one of the most suppressed and misunderstood emotions: ANGER.

Women are often taught to mask anger with politeness, perfectionism, or compliance. Here, we don’t push it away, apologize for it, or attempt to fix it. We embrace it, express it physically, and channel it together into creative energy and unshakable boundaries.

🔥 Embodied Practice

Movement, breathwork, boxing elements, improv, and vocal release to move bound energy out of the body safely.

✨ What You Experience

Shift from "I am angry or overwhelmed" to "I feel vibrant, clear on my boundaries, and fully alive."

The name Pretty & Angry is inspired by the Polish saying "Złość piękności szkodzi": literally, "Anger harms beauty." For generations, many girls grew up hearing this phrase whenever they expressed frustration or anger, learning that to be "pretty" they should not express anger.
